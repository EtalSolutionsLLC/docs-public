6. ReviewNudge-Specific Processing

ReviewNudge helps businesses request and manage customer reviews. A ReviewNudge customer may provide a recipient's contact information and direct ReviewNudge to send a request or follow-up on the customer's behalf.

Et al uses Customer Business Data only as needed to provide ReviewNudge, comply with documented customer instructions and our agreements, secure the platform, prevent misuse, comply with law, and maintain or improve the reliability of the Service. We do not use Customer Business Data for unrelated advertising.

ReviewNudge is not a general-purpose bulk-email platform. Customers must have an appropriate reason and legal basis to contact each recipient. Customers may not use purchased, scraped, or unlawfully obtained lists, impersonate another person or business, conceal the source of a communication, evade suppression requests, or bypass sending limits.

  • Recipients may contact the business that sent the request regarding the underlying relationship, message, or use of their information.
  • Recipients may contact Et al regarding ReviewNudge platform privacy or security practices.
  • Where an opt-out or suppression mechanism is provided, we may process the resulting preference to prevent additional messages through the applicable customer workspace.

7. Email Delivery, Communications, and Abuse Prevention

We use email-delivery and related providers to transmit service communications. Those providers may process recipient addresses, message content, sender information, and delivery metadata. We may retrieve and store provider message identifiers and delivery outcomes to support troubleshooting, reporting, suppression, security, and policy enforcement.

We may use automated and manual controls to verify accounts, rate-limit activity, reserve sending capacity, identify suspicious patterns, restrict or suspend sending, and protect recipients and the Services. Relevant signals may include account age, verification status, workspace state, message volume, recipient repetition, recipient-domain patterns, delivery failures, complaints, and attempts to evade limits.

These controls are designed to prevent abuse and protect the platform. They are not intended to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about individuals solely through automated processing. A legitimate customer may contact support to request review of a restriction.
