Version: 1.0

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Purpose

This list identifies third-party providers Et al Solutions LLC, DBA SIMPLIFAI ("Et al") currently uses to operate, secure, support, bill for, and measure its services. A provider is a Subprocessor only when it processes Personal Data on behalf of Et al's customer. Some providers also act as independent controllers for activities such as payment-network compliance or their own account administration.

The data actually shared depends on the service, product configuration, customer choices, consent, and the event being processed. Et al seeks to limit information to what the provider needs for its defined purpose.

Third-Party Review Services

Products may connect to third-party review sites selected by the customer. For ReviewNudge, a customer supplies a review destination and the product may retrieve public review information from a supported provider. The third party's own terms and privacy practices govern its service. ReviewNudge does not send the customer's contact list to a review provider for matching.

Changes

Et al may add, replace, or remove providers as products and operating requirements change. This page will be updated when a provider begins or stops processing Personal Data for a material service function.

A customer with a legally required objection to a new Subprocessor may contact privacy@etal.solutions. The applicable agreement and Data Processing Addendum govern the response.

Contact

Questions about a provider, data category, or product-specific processing path may be sent to privacy@etal.solutions. Do not send passwords, private keys, complete payment-card numbers, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Scope: This is a current operational disclosure, not a representation that every provider receives every category of information listed above.
